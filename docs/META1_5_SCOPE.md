# META1.5 scope and frozen boundary

## Decision task

META1.5 is a prior-art collision audit, not a second Development search. Its only question is whether selecting a partial factorial measurement set for signed order>=3 support recovery in a coherent AND/Mobius dictionary under replicated biological responses remains a technically defensible research problem.

## Frozen parent state

- Base commit: `79204a06e5079b6167153edf03cdb8ee0dcda514`.
- Parent decision: `D-B PROMISING`.
- Development candidate: `hybrid_d50 + ElasticNet`.
- Conditional regime: Ishizawa, six companion factors, at least five biological replicates per complete-panel cell, log10 CFU, budgets 16/24/32 and at most 50% distinct communities.
- Primary estimand: signed support of order>=3 Mobius coefficients under the frozen META1 threshold rule.
- Development estimate: macro F1 0.2001 to 0.2509, difference +0.0508. This is outcome-adaptive Development evidence, not an unbiased confirmation effect.
- Negative scope evidence: Diaz-Colunga did not replicate the gain.

## Prohibited actions in this phase

- No execution, inspection, regeneration, or equivalent use of reserve seeds 505 or 606.
- No change to the D-opt fraction, 50% regime boundary, response scale, support threshold, estimand, panels, or budgets.
- No model training and no new regime search.
- No rewriting of META1 conclusions.
- No claim that held-out masks on reused landscapes are external validation.

## Audit distinctions

The review separates: prediction-optimal design; coefficient-estimation design; sparse support/sign-recovery design; sparse Mobius query algorithms; and FDR/inference-aware interaction discovery. A new biological application alone is not a technical contribution.

## Search accounting

The targeted search screened 34 candidate works and deep-reviewed 17 directly relevant works spanning support-aware design, supersaturated/fractional factorial design, sparse Mobius recovery, noisy support recovery, and interaction inference. The complete extraction is in `META1_5_CONTRIBUTION_COLLISION_MATRIX.md` and the bibliography is in `references/meta1_5_references.bib`.

## Outcome

The unique audit decision is **META1.5-B — MODIFY**. The broad design-aware/sign-recovery direction is occupied. A narrower fixed-row, unknown-support, order-targeted AND-dictionary design problem remains plausible but is not yet a confirmed contribution. Because this changes the intervention materially, the current reserve is not valid for the modified candidate.
