## New evidence checked

- Recent GHD/FHD approximation, FPT, rerootability and query-learning work is
  genuinely new external evidence, but it creates a stronger theory program
  rather than reopening the frozen ML formulations.
- Recent hypergraph projection recovery results sharpen identifiability under
  random-model assumptions. They do not correct S3.0's factual result about
  natural observation and independent real gold.
- Recent HGNN generalization bounds do not make native width theory necessary
  and do not satisfy an empirical classical-residual gate.

**Result: zero frozen reopening conditions are currently satisfied.**
