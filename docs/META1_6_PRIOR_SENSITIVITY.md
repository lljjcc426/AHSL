# META1.6 prior sensitivity

The frozen designs are rescored under four outcome-independent ensembles: primary, sparser, denser, and weak-signal. The table reports objective mean, CVaR20, and integrated singular fraction for HILS, the full candidate, D-opt, and uniform at each budget.

Results are in `results/meta1_6/aggregated/prior_sensitivity.csv`. The candidate's absolute score and relative ranking vary materially with density and signal strength. This is evidence that the objective is prior-sensitive; it is not legitimate to select a new Pi using Ishizawa support frequencies to reverse the result.
