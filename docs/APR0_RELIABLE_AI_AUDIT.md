# AP-R0 reliable-AI audit

AutomationBench provides programmatically verifiable business workflows, while
SWE-bench Live, AgentRx, Spider 2.0 and live SQL benchmarks sharpen repository,
debugging and enterprise execution. These are materially better targets than a
generic chat-agent benchmark.

The stable problems are semantic mis-specification, state/recovery, diagnosis
cost and validation scalability. They would remain meaningful under better
foundation models. However, the executable comparison still needs a frozen
current agent/model and its actual tool traces. Public paper results or a 2025
cached generation cannot establish a 2026 residual.

The prior AP0 enterprise-data route remains closed. CHESS, CHASE-SQL,
Spider-Agent, execution guidance and CEDAR already occupy retrieval,
candidate-selection, verifier and cost-aware validation. No current run exposed
a specific sound/complete repair gap under a declared specification language.

Consequently B1/B2 fail G5. They are not HOLD finalists: current-baseline access
is only one problem; novelty also collides with verifier/retry/agent frameworks.
