# S3.0 Classical Methods Audit

## Projection and latent groups

Maximal clique enumeration, minimum/weighted clique cover, Bayesian/MDL
hypergraph reconstruction, stochastic block models, mixed-membership SBMs,
nonnegative matrix factorization, and overlapping community detection all map
pairwise evidence to group explanations. The inverse is nonunique; priors select
a representative. COLT 2024/2025 gives exact/partial recovery thresholds for
sparse random uniform hypergraphs, so even the clean theory interface is active.

## Dynamics and chemical reaction networks

SINDy, sparse polynomial regression, ARNI, Granger/transfer-entropy approaches,
reactionet lasso, and classical nonlinear system identification recover feature
supports or reaction graphs. Sample complexity depends on sparsity, feature
coherence, noise, derivative estimation and excitation. A hyperedge is the
support of a multivariate term. When coupling functions decompose or data remain
near a fixed point, the high-order support is not identifiable.

## Pooled measurements

Combinatorial group testing and compressed sensing already supply disjunct
designs, sparse recovery algorithms, noise models, and `m = O(s log p)`-type
theory. Pooled CRISPR interaction scoring is handled with GLMs, variational
models and contrasts. The scientifically meaningful challenge is assay design
and effect definition, not hypergraph recovery.

## Complex discovery

MCL, MCODE and ClusterONE are mature graph-to-set algorithms; ClusterONE
explicitly recovers overlapping complexes. CF-MS practice combines multiple
co-elution scores, supervised pair classification and clustering. FDR-controlled
targeted methods such as mCP and large integrated resources such as hu.MAP3.0
further reduce method headroom.

## Margins and censoring

IPF, maximum entropy, hierarchical/marginal log-linear models and partial-
identification bounds are the correct tools for low-order marginal releases.
Capture-recapture and occupancy models address imperfect detection. Their key
unknown is the censoring mechanism; adding a neural set decoder does not identify
an MNAR event roster.

The surviving real problems are already inverse problems with mature estimands
and theory. No classical failure tied to independent high-order gold was found.
