# S3.0 Modern ML Audit

## Projection reconstruction

- **From Graphs to Hypergraphs (ICLR 2024)** proves combinatorial information
  loss without extra help and learns with application-domain prior knowledge on
  eight projected real hypergraphs.
- **MARIOH (2025)** uses projection multiplicity, provable size-2 filtering, a
  classifier and bidirectional candidate search on ten datasets.
- **COLT 2024/2025** gives recovery/impossibility thresholds for random
  `d`-uniform hypergraphs, including weighted projections.

This is a saturated modern/theoretical line under artificial projection
benchmarks. Natural pairwise-only data lack independent event-set gold.

## Dynamics

THIS (Nature Communications 2025), the algebraic reconstruction of Malizia et
al. (Nature Communications 2024), the Kramers–Moyal method in PRX 2024,
Bayes-THIS (2026), and multiplex regression extensions (2026) already cover
sparse, Bayesian, directed/weighted and multiplex variants. Exact structural
accuracy remains synthetic; real EEG supplies downstream/interpretive evidence.

## Pooled and latent variables

NAIAD (ICML 2025) and contemporary combinatorial CRISPR methods predict genetic
interaction effects and guide experimental selection. Tensor-rank and spectral
latent-variable methods already infer latent components under completeness,
multi-view, rank or expansion assumptions. These are effect/latent-factor
models, not missing hyperedge identities with independent gold.

## Protein complexes

hu.MAP3.0 integrates more than 25,000 proteomic experiments to produce over
15,000 predicted human complexes. EPIC/mCF-MS pipelines, CORUM-integrated
benchmark refinement, deep pair scoring and mCP's FDR-controlled workflow make
the domain methodologically mature. Their continuing difficulty is incomplete,
context-dependent gold and experimental coverage.

## Residual classification

The residuals are benchmark provenance, sensing design, assay-disjoint
validation and partial identification. None is an isolated need for a new
structure learner. No PyTorch model or new latent-variable method was built.
