# META1.6 scope and frozen protocol

META1.6 asks whether a fixed subset of rows from the `d=6` Boolean cube can improve signed support recovery for the 42 order-3-or-higher AND/Möbius terms when 21 order-1/2 terms are nuisance. Budgets are 16, 24, and 32; the empty row is mandatory. The primary real metric is landscape-macro signed high-order F1 on Ishizawa, paired over Development seeds 101/202/303/404.

The phase freezes homoscedastic noise, an outcome-independent support ensemble, Lasso/KKT Route B, and a fixed-row exchange search before real outcomes are evaluated. Díaz remains a negative diagnostic. Replicate-aware allocation, FDR-oriented method changes, neural search, response-scale rescue, and new biological panels are out of scope.

Retired reserve seeds 505/606 are forbidden. This phase neither constructs nor runs a new reserve. The stop rule is D-C when the modified candidate fails both HILS on synthetic recovery and HILS on real Development F1.
