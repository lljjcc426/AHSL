# S3.0 Literature Landscape

| landscape | natural observation? | direct real structural gold? | identifiability | classical/modern state | outcome |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| graph projection -> hyperedges | sometimes | no convincing paired route | non-injective; conditional theory | Bayesian/MDL, ICLR 2024, COLT thresholds, MARIOH | kill |
| dynamics -> couplings | yes | no; real evidence indirect | conditional on nonlinear excitation and model sufficiency | SINDy plus several 2024–26 methods | kill |
| pooled assay -> effects | yes | same-assay/partial positives | classical sparse-design conditions | compressed sensing, GLM, NAIAD | kill |
| censored groups | yes | no scalable full-event gold | severe under unknown MNAR censoring | capture-recapture/latent groups | kill |
| indirect assay -> complexes | yes | partial curated positives | nonunique/context dependent | graph clustering, NMF, hu.MAP3.0/mCP | kill |
| marginals -> dependencies | yes | generally unavailable by design | non-injective | log-linear/max-entropy/tensor methods | kill |

The literature contains strong theory where structural truth is synthetic, and
strong real measurements where structural truth is incomplete. The intersection
required by S3.0—natural partiality, independent set-level gold, identifiability,
and a residual beyond standard inverse models—was not found.
