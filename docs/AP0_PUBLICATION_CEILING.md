# AP0 publication ceiling

## Dynamic/incremental recursive query execution

- **Minimal publishable:** one reproducible execution/state optimization, exact outputs, modern open baselines and meaningful improvements across two workload families; likely ICDE/EDBT or a focused systems venue depending on breadth.
- **Strong:** a principled update-sensitive algorithm integrated into a real engine, covering insertions and deletions, multiple recursion patterns, with tail latency and state analysis; credible SIGMOD/PVLDB.
- **Top ceiling:** general mechanism or optimizer with correctness, complexity/amortized-state insight, broad LDBC/Datalog/program-analysis evidence and superiority to FlowLog/Feldera/Kuzu-class baselines.
- The field routinely publishes algorithms, systems, empirical benchmarks and theory, so the program can produce distinct contribution types without slicing one result.

## Reliable enterprise SQL workflows

A minimal prompting/agent improvement has a low durable ceiling. A strong contribution needs a new semantic verification/repair abstraction and end-to-end task completion across Spider 2.0 and LiveSQLBench. Top venues require more than leaderboard accuracy: contamination control, cost, executable validity, strong current models and generalization.

## Dynamic vector-relational execution

The ceiling is high, as ACORN/VBASE/SIEVE demonstrate, but the entry bar is now equally high. A new paper needs a workload dimension not solved by DIGRA/RangePQ/CONDA/SIEVE and a mature implementation; another graph index with selective filters is not enough.

## Constraint and scheduling families

CP supports deep algorithms/theory and applications, but a generic solver enhancement is outside AP0's application-first objective. Scheduling can reach strong domain/CP venues only with a credible public real workload and superiority to CP-SAT/domain hybrids.
